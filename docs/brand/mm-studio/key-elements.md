## Guardrails carried over from v1 (keep)

- AI-assisted scanning supports assessment and consultation; it is **not** medical diagnosis.
- Exosome / HLA-G concepts are mechanism education, **not** proven clinical efficacy; no absolute claims (zero redness, zero risk, zero downtime).
- Financials are management data, unaudited; funding figures marked `£XXX` remain placeholders until confirmed.
- No guaranteed qualification, job, visa, immigration outcome or investment return.
- AI/data roadmap items are **proposed future development**, not shipped features, unless the owner confirms otherwise.
- Skin/scalp images are personal (potentially special-category) data under UK GDPR: explicit consent, minimisation, and a clear retention policy.

## Guardrails added from the Seoul memo (keep)

- UK-originated materials (deck, brochure, booking pack, speaker notes) never name treatments, products or medicines and never use outcome words (lifting, firming, glass skin, tone-up, hair growth, tightening, contouring, regeneration as a result). Prescription-only medicines may not be advertised to the UK public, wherever the treatment is given. Generic modality categories may appear in this archive as presenter background only.
- Speaker notes are presenter material: strip them before the .pptx is sent to anyone outside the owner's team.
- Seoul treatments are given in Korea, subject to clinical suitability; MM Studio UK is non-medical; "physician-led" only once WBI confirms who administers each item; no outcome is claimed; exosome items in a clinic programme are clinic protocols, not evidence (in vitro only).
- Seoul Day 3 and 4 sessions sit within the study-visit component; they are not the 12-month certificate course and confer no qualification. Avoid "academy certificate", "accredited", "diploma", "CPD".
- Use "exclusive" only if WBI confirms the access is not offered to other groups; until then "being arranged (to be confirmed)". Name speakers and print dates only once confirmed.
- Keep "franchisee", "golden opportunity", "fast-growing", "wow", "retreat", "beauty holiday", "investment seminar", "lightest and fastest option" and any ranking of routes by speed or ease off slides; the three operating formats and the UK unit are the documented routes.
- No referral or introducer benefit appears in investor material: a benefit for bringing in someone who may subscribe for a unit that includes equity is a UK financial-promotion question and needs legal advice first. If a referral benefit is ever used for a stand-alone trip sold to non-investors: in kind only, in booking terms, never credited against units or equity.
- Day 4 business and investment Q&A with UK participants is a financial-promotion context: take advice before those sessions, use approved or exempt documents only, and keep WBI's own US$2.0M round and franchise forecasts out of UK sessions.
- Deck cross-references in speaker notes use slide titles, not numbers.
