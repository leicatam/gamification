## 2. Mission, vision, values

### Mission

Equip Hong Kong's pre-retirement professionals, mid-career pivots and active
retirees to incorporate and operate an AI-augmented one-person company —
adaptively, patiently, and without surrendering professional judgement to
automation.

### Vision

A generation of credible one-person corporates that convert decades of
expertise into durable, dignified income. The smallest unit of viable
incorporation shrinks to one human plus an agent stack — and that human
remains in charge of every decision that matters.

### Values

1. **Precision over hype.** Every claim is HK-specific, sourced, and
   defensible. We name the Cap. 622 section, the IRD form, the MPF rule.
2. **Patient by default.** No streaks, no shame, no time pressure. Mastery is
   measured against the six-arc readiness score, not against other learners.
3. **Human-in-charge.** Drafts only. No agent in this system files anything
   to the Companies Registry, IRD, MPFA, or any bank without explicit human
   approval.
4. **HK-anchored.** Cap. 622 is the source of truth, not generic global
   incorporation content. The product is built for Hong Kong first.
5. **Dignity, not dopamine.** The active-retiree persona is a first-class
   citizen. Accessibility defaults, voice I/O, and explicit pause points are
   built in — not bolted on.

## 3. Positioning

### Category

Adaptive AI tutor + operational agent toolkit for the one-person Hong Kong
private limited company.

### Frame of reference

| Alternative | What it gives | What it misses |
|---|---|---|
| Generic incorporation services (TMF, Osome, Sleek, etc.) | Filings, secretary, audit | No learning layer; no agent runbook; no adaptation to persona |
| Generic AI tutors (Khan-style, MOOC) | Adaptive pedagogy | No HK Cap. 622 specificity; no operational layer |
| Personal accountant / company secretary | Authoritative advice | Expensive; not 24/7; not designed for AI-native operation |
| DIY (forums + IRD/CR websites) | Free | High abandonment; no adaptation; no guardrails |

### Where OPC Tutor sits

Between the learning layer and the doing layer. Phase 1 graduates the learner
to operator-readiness. Phase 2 (agent toolkit) operates alongside the human,
draft-only, under explicit human-in-the-loop guardrails.

### Single sentence

OPC Tutor is the only product that teaches you how to run a Hong Kong
one-person Ltd _and_ ships the agent stack that runs the draft work for you —
without ever filing on your behalf.

## 4. Voice & tone

### Identity (from the tutor system prompt)

> Precise, warm, and direct. Behaves like a respected, patient
> subject-matter teacher. Does not perform like a chatbot mascot.

### Voice principles

- **Name the limit.** When a learner asks a question that crosses into advice,
  say so. "I can explain the two-tier rate. I can't tell you whether your
  Singapore client revenue is offshore-sourced — that's a CPA call."
- **Define every HK acronym** on first use, regardless of audience: CR, IRD,
  BR, NNC1, NAR1, SCR, MPF, BIR51.
- **Adapt length to persona.** Mid-career pivot: terse. Pre-retirement:
  substantive paragraphs. Active retiree: short paragraphs with explicit
  pause points.
- **Never gamify with points or streaks.** Progress is a readiness score
  against the six arcs. Failure is never penalized.
- **No exclamation marks** in product copy unless quoting a learner.

### Tone matrix

| Surface | Tone |
|---|---|
| Marketing site headlines | Direct, confident, low theatre |
| Onboarding dialogue | Warm, curious, accessibility-first |
| Tutor replies | Socratic, precise, paragraph-shaped |
| Compliance reminders | Calm, factual, deadline-anchored |
| Error / agent-mistake recovery | Plain, owning the error, with a fix path |
| Investor / partner comms | Substantive, HK-anchored, no AI-hype superlatives |

### Words we use

incorporate · operate · readiness · artifact · draft · review · approve ·
human-in-the-loop · adapt · pace · pause · mastery · audit · CPA · jurisdiction

### Words we avoid

unlock · revolutionize · disrupt · effortless · 10x · streak · level up ·
gamify (as a verb — we _are_ gamified, we don't gamify users) · autopilot ·
fully automated · "your AI accountant"

## 5. Logo

### Concept

A wordmark **OPC** with a single dot above the **P** representing the one
human director-shareholder around which the company is structured. Optional
secondary lockup adds **Tutor** in a lighter weight to the right.

```
            ·
   O   P   C   Tutor
```

A circular monogram variant — an **O** enclosing a centered dot — is reserved
for app icons and favicons.

### Construction

- The dot above the P sits at cap-height, diameter = stroke width of the P.
- Letter spacing: optical, not metric. Tighten OP, loosen PC.
- Clear space on all sides: equal to the height of the dot.

### Don'ts

- Don't apply gradients, shadows, or bevels.
- Don't rotate or skew.
- Don't reproduce on busy photographic backgrounds without the bone or
  charcoal scrim.
- Don't pair with another logo at a larger size in co-branding lockups.

> The logo lockup itself is a design deliverable that this manual specifies
> but does not draw. Hand the specification to a designer or generate vector
> from a tool that emits SVG; this manual is the canonical brief.

## 6. Colour

The palette is built for long-form reading at large type sizes (the
active-retiree default is extra-large) and for high contrast against bone
and charcoal surfaces.

| Role | Name | HEX | RGB | Use |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Primary | Ink Blue | `#0E2A47` | 14, 42, 71 | Headlines, primary buttons, brand bar |
| Secondary | Jade | `#2E7D6B` | 46, 125, 107 | Progress, success states, HK accent |
| Accent | Amber | `#D89B3A` | 216, 155, 58 | Mastery moments, highlights, callouts |
| Surface | Bone | `#F6F1E7` | 246, 241, 231 | Page background, document feel |
| Text | Charcoal | `#1A1A1A` | 26, 26, 26 | Body text on bone |
| Divider | Mist | `#E4E9EE` | 228, 233, 238 | Hairlines, dividers, low-emphasis cards |
| Warning | Brick | `#A8412F` | 168, 65, 47 | Risk callouts, audit deadlines |

### Contrast rules

- Body text must hit WCAG AA against its background (4.5:1) at default size,
  AAA (7:1) at extra-large size.
- Amber is never a body-text colour. Reserved for accent only.
- Jade is the only colour permitted for progress and success states.

## 7. Typography

### Type system

| Role | Family | Weights | Fallback |
|---|---|---|---|
| Display / Headline | IBM Plex Serif | 600, 700 | Source Serif 4, Georgia |
| Body | Inter | 400, 500, 600 | system-ui, Helvetica |
| Traditional Chinese display | Noto Serif HK | 600 | PingFang TC |
| Traditional Chinese body | Noto Sans HK | 400, 500 | PingFang TC |
| Monospace (forms, code, filings) | IBM Plex Mono | 400, 500 | Menlo |

### Scale (px, 1rem = 16px)

| Token | Default | Large | Extra-large |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| `h1` | 40 | 48 | 56 |
| `h2` | 32 | 36 | 42 |
| `h3` | 24 | 28 | 32 |
| `body` | 16 | 20 | 24 |
| `caption` | 13 | 16 | 18 |

The active-retiree persona launches at **extra-large** by default. Type sizing
is a brand-level commitment, not a setting hidden behind preferences.

### Rules

- Headlines: serif, sentence case, no hard line breaks unless poetic.
- Body: sans, generous line-height (1.55–1.7).
- Numbers in tables: tabular figures (`font-variant-numeric: tabular-nums`).
- HK acronyms: kept in caps on first use, expanded inline.

## 8. Imagery & illustration

### What we show

- Human hands at work — calligraphy, ledgers, a laptop next to a kettle.
- HK at human scale — Sheung Wan small-business streets, wet markets, the
  Companies Registry building, MTR stations — not Central megabank atria.
- Working artifacts — annotated NNC1 drafts, a chart of accounts, a decision
  log screenshot — not marketing-grade abstract graphics.

### What we don't show

- Stock photography of "happy retirees on a beach."
- Robot hands or anthropomorphised AI imagery.
- Stack-of-coins / dollar-sign cliches.
- Aspirational drone shots of Victoria Harbour as a stand-in for the product.

### Illustration

Line illustrations in ink blue on bone, with jade or amber single-colour
accents. Stroke weight matches the wordmark's stroke. Never use gradients.

## 9. Motion & sound

- **Transitions** ≤ 200ms, eased. Never block learner input.
- **Audio cues** are functional only — no celebratory chimes for completing a
  section. The product is not a slot machine.
- **Voice I/O** uses a single warm HK English voice; an additional Cantonese
  voice ships in Phase 2.
- **Auto-advance is off by default** for the active-retiree persona.

## 11. Brand pillars (the elevator version)

1. **Adaptive.** Three personas. Three pacing defaults. Vocabulary, length,
   examples, and accessibility tuned per learner per turn.
2. **HK-anchored.** Cap. 622, IRD, MPFA, two-tier profits tax, BR / NAR1 /
   BIR51 — the canonical references, never genericised.
3. **Agent-augmented, human-in-charge.** Draft work delegated; every filing,
   bank action, and signed deliverable is the human's call.
4. **Educational, not advisory.** We never claim or imply legal, tax, or
   accounting authority. We make qualified HK professionals look better, not
   redundant.
5. **Dignified gamification.** Mastery without shame. No streaks, no points,
   no leaderboards. Progress is a private readiness score.

## 12. Things the brand will not do

- Ship an "autofile to IRD" button.
- Run AI-generated stock photography of "the future of work".
- Use streaks, badges, or leaderboards in the learner UI.
- Position itself as a replacement for a HK CPA or company secretary.
- Use the word "disrupt" in any external surface.
- Localize away from Hong Kong as the anchor jurisdiction in v1.
