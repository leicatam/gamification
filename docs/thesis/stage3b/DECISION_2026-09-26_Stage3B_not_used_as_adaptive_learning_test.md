# Decision record — Stage 3B withdrawn as the adaptive-learning test (26 September 2026)

**Author:** Sidney K. T. Tam (EngD candidate). Recorded from the author's written instruction of 26 September 2026 to the thesis-support session; this file is the dated repository artifact that thesis §8.8, Table 3.1A, Table 9.1 and Appendix I cite.

## The author's instruction (verbatim)

> "After the initial round of Stage 3B test, the measurement of the learning ability was not articulated in line with the concept of adaptive learning ... we stopped using the stage 3B to determine the adaptive learning process happening in the AI enabled zero-failure game mechanism. Therefore, we decided to conclude the thesis based on the finding of FIAS and the adaptive learning process as a discussion and future study. Hence please use the stage 3B data which we have achieved, and revise the thesis ... and the paper 3 for submission too. Please check if it is necessary to add Stage 3B data and parameters, tables and graphs to the thesis or create appendix to conclude the work."

## What the decision changes

1. The thesis concludes on the FIAS finding of Stages 1–3A (Stage-1 survey; Stage-2 action research; Stage-3A between-cohort decoupling).
2. Adaptive learning is carried as a design direction (Chapter 7) for discussion and future study; it is not reported as tested, demonstrated or discovered.
3. Stage 3B is reported as an achieved, descriptive component-study record at a stated cut-off of **26 September 2026** (thesis Appendix I; §8.8; Table 3.1A; Table 9.1): pre-specified (repository-dated 8–9 August 2026) 2×2 design; builds of 9 and 13 August; frozen study edition of 15 September; one verified return (15 August); five returns of 15 September excluded; no clean-run return; the Pro v4.1 researcher self-test Batch 01 (19–20 September). No confirmatory analysis is reported and no results cell is left open.
4. Reason recorded: the learning measurement available in the Stage-3B record was not articulated to the adaptive-learning construct — immediate task performance under assistance is not durable learning; the fixed-course checks sit near ceiling on non-equivalent course forms; the adaptive arm is a support package (difficulty adjustment with coaching) rather than difficulty adjustment alone; assignment persisted per browser profile and the first-exposure build differed across returns; the only performance series is a researcher self-test of unverified identity; and no retention or transfer measure exists.

## What the decision does not change

- The frozen study edition (FROZEN20260915) and its hosted links remain as recorded; the clean-run window stated in FREEZE_RECORD.md (15 September – 15 October 2026) is not reopened or extended by this note. Any return received after the cut-off is archived as received and may be reported only as a dated addendum outside the thesis evidence base; the thesis makes no provision for it.
- No Stage-3B file is used as evidence for FIAS, for reduced abandonment, or for any adaptive-learning effect.
- The term "pre-registered" is not applied to Stage 3B anywhere; the study is "pre-specified (repository-dated)".
