separate from the original Stage 3B cohort and from the thesis evidence supporting the provisional FIAS account.

What the routes reveal
Five of twelve visits contain no practice run. Four consist only of a closing-labelled check: P8P2Z9 visit 2, PU5K58 visits 1–2
and PEVNZ8 visit 2. PEVNZ8 visit 3 contains a 1.473-second warmup stopped before any obstacle, followed by a closing
check. These records must not be treated as five completed adaptive-learning sequences. The files do not establish why
those routes occurred. [S03, S06, S08]
Two of the seven visits containing practice lack a closing check: P8P2Z9 visit 3 and PU5K58 visit 3. Therefore, 5/7 practice-
containing visits, or 5/12 software visits overall, have an opening–closing comparison. This is measurement availability, not
adherence or effectiveness. [S03, S06]
Recorded gaps vary from 3.392 seconds to 11 hours 14 minutes 7.749 seconds. PEVNZ8’s two restarts occur after 9.484
and 3.392 seconds; these are not evidence of separate-day retention. Four restart records say self_initiated and three say
not_reported; this describes the recorded response, not independently verified absence of prompting. [S03, S06, S08]

Stopping is not automatically abandonment
Fifteen of seventeen practice runs reached their planned time limit; two P8P2Z9 runs ended at the three-bump limit. Both
were followed by another practice run in the same visit. Twelve visit-ended events are present. Neither a game-over event,
a skipped questionnaire nor a completed visit establishes later abandonment or FIAS. No prospective non-return
observation window is supplied. [S03, S06, S08–S10]

Case interpretation
P8P2Z9’s first practice ended after 6.773 seconds with three hits in four obstacles. The next run was accepted at a lower
pace and lasted 29.093 seconds, also ending at the bump limit. A subsequent visibility response was followed by a route-
guided run at the same pace, with no hits in 35 obstacles. This is a useful within-record adaptation sequence; changes in
exposure, support and repeated practice prevent attribution of the better immediate performance to one cause. [S03]
PT8FJC’s progression from 1.20 to 1.60 should not be described as four AI-selected increases. Three were explicit player
overrides and one retained a local-rule increase. In the first transition, the neutral reason speed co-occurred with right and
okay, yet generated an easier offline proposal. This supports reviewing the directional meaning of the feedback options.
[S10]
Not every pace change uses post-game feedback: warmup calibration determines initial practice settings. P8P2Z9 visit 4
begins practice at 0.74 without a decision ID. The logbook therefore separates warmup-setting, local-rules and player-
choice rather than treating every adjustment as Jev reasoning. [S03]

Suggested wording for your academic review
“The initial Stage 3B Pro export batch comprised ten files representing five stored player codes and twelve software-
recorded visits. Deduplication identified 42 distinct runs, including 17 practice runs. Ten answered feedback records were
linked to ten applied next-run decisions, comprising seven final local-rule decisions and three player overrides. Five within-
visit fixed-course comparisons showed mixed changes: two improvements, two declines and one unchanged result. The
dataset supports the operation of an auditable feedback-informed practice mechanism and identifies important navigation
and measurement gaps. As the exports are labelled researcher self-test, contain no live Jev inference and do not establish
independent participant identities or a longitudinal follow-up design, no claim of durable learning, reduced abandonment
or FIAS validation is made.” [S01–S10; derived logbook]

Relationship to the thesis
Retain this batch as a separate Pro development appendix or progress-review item. It does not replace the original Stage
3B dataset, strengthen an earlier causal FIAS claim automatically, or require reopening the thesis with a new efficacy trial.
A useful engineering contribution here is the traceability from measured actions and reported experience to chosen
settings, together with the explicit limits discovered through use.
No game code, thesis draft, manuscript or existing review deck was modified in preparing this logbook. The Confirmations sheet is
intentionally incomplete where the exports cannot supply the answer.
