## How it is read in the thesis (item G.16)
- A secondary, centre-side record: it shows that the register's values for these ten participants have a counterpart in the centre's notes as transcribed by a therapist. It is not a contemporaneous original and not a session-by-session log: each entry carries the participant's final cycle count and final index under a single date (including 11 July, the first day), so the dates' meaning (first attendance, last attendance or date of the note) is not established.
- The legend's reversed direction (a falling index = improvement) is the same error later identified in the archived data file's legend and corrected in the archive documentation (§5.5); the error therefore appears to have entered at the centre. On the InBody instrument a higher composite score is better; the page's own notes ("self-reports improvement; satisfied") are consistent with that.
- The transcription carries symptom and self-report notes per participant. The thesis's account (§3.4) that symptom entries were attributed by the researcher from field notes is retained; whether the transcription or the field notes was the source for each entry is not recorded.
