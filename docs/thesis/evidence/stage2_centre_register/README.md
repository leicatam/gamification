## Properties that bear on how it is read
- Each entry carries the participant's final cycle count and final 'after' index under a single date, including on 11 July, the first day of the window. The page is therefore a per-participant summary compiled at or after the participant's last play, not a session-by-session log, whatever its preamble says; the date against each entry is of uncertain meaning (first attendance, last attendance or compilation date) until the centre says.
- The legend's direction (a falling index = improvement) is the same direction error later identified in the archived data file's legend and corrected in the archive documentation (§5.5); on the InBody instrument a higher composite score is better. The error therefore appears to originate at the centre. The page's notes ("self-reports improvement; satisfied") are consistent with the index direction being the reverse of the legend.
- The session dates do not appear in any record received before 3 October 2026 and cannot be checked against another source.
- The thesis (§3.4, on the author's account of 2 October 2026) states that the therapists kept no written per-participant records and that symptom entries were attributed by the researcher from field notes. This page is a written per-participant record from the centre carrying symptom and self-report notes. The two accounts are reconciled in the thesis only when the author states when the photocopy was obtained and whether these notes, rather than his field notes, were the source of the symptom entries.
- The handwriting is highly uniform; the page is read as a photocopy on the author's statement.
