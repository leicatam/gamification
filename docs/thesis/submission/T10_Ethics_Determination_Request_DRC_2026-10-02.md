HSEARS20260805008 (PI: Prof. Chi Fai Cheung; approved 6 August 2026 for 16 June 2024 to 30 October 2026) covers the EngD research of Sidney K. T. Tam. The approval was granted after the field stages had been conducted. Before the thesis is submitted on 15 October 2026, we would be grateful for written determinations on the following points, so that the thesis can report the Committee's position rather than an open question:

1. Coverage. Does the approval cover the procedures of Stage 1 (survey, July–August 2024), Stage 2 (facility deployment, June–August 2025), Stage 3 (remote deployment, April–July 2026) and the expert consultations, as described in the application?

2. Stage 2 consent. The arrangements were as follows:
   - Residents were not told during the deployment that play formed part of research, because announcing a fall-risk study would have re-imposed the clinical frame under study.
   - Gatekeeper consent was given by the facility's general manager.
   - Written consent was obtained retrospectively, and only from the nine participants who completed the cycle.
   - The 21 who stopped did not sign, and their facility-recorded data are used in aggregate.
   Does the Committee accept these arrangements, and does it require a stated limitation on the use of the non-signing participants' data?

3. Stage 3 consent. Consent was implied by returning the game results and the questionnaire.
   - The invitation on file is a reconstruction dated 4 August 2026.
   - The returned materials were compiled into an aggregate summary in July 2026 and were not retained as individual records.
   Does the Committee accept implied consent on this documentation?

4. Stage 3B (August–October 2026). Did the Stage 3B collection fall within the approved scope, or did it require an amendment? It used an in-game consent screen citing the approval reference.

5. Expert consultations. Two consultations took place before the experts signed consent: one in March 2026 with consent signed on 4 August 2026, and one in June 2026 with consent signed on 10 August 2026. Is this acceptable, given that the experts have since consented and will confirm their summaries?

A short written reply by 12 October 2026 would allow the determinations to be stated in the thesis. Copies of the application, the approval memo and the relevant records can be provided on request.
