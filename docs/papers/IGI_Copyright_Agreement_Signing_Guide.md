## How it is actually signed
Per IGI Global's current journal guidelines, the corresponding author
signs the Author's Warranty and Transfer of Copyright **digitally inside
the eEditorial Discovery submission system** — standalone PDF agreements
are no longer accepted for journal submissions. The PDF form is the same
text; use it to read the terms in advance. (The PDF's own footer offers
email/postal signing; the online guidelines supersede it for journals.)

- Copyright in the article text transfers to IGI Global. You KEEP the
  intellectual property — FIDS/FIAS, the models, theories and
  conclusions — and may rewrite and reuse the ideas elsewhere, but NOT
  the verbatim article text without IGI's written permission.
- No royalties; you indemnify the publisher against copyright/libel
  claims; Pennsylvania law governs.
- Fair Use Policy: you may post the final typeset PDF to your own
  secure personal site or the university repository; you may NOT post
  it to ResearchGate, Academia.edu, SSRN, arXiv, etc.
