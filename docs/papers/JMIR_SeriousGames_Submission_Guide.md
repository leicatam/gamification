## What the manuscript now contains

- Title with study type suffix ("… Action Research Study" — JMIR convention).
- Structured abstract, 429 words (limit 450): Background / Objective /
  Methods / Results / Conclusions.
- IMRD structure: Introduction (Background, Research Questions, Prior
  Work, Objectives and Contribution) → Methods (Study Design, Ethical
  Considerations, Data Governance, Site and Participants, Definitions,
  the Gamified Intervention, Data Collection, Data Verification and
  Withdrawal Statement, Statistical Analysis) → Results → Discussion
  (incl. Limitations, Practical Implications, Future Work, Conclusions).
- Figures 1–6 embedded in text with sequential captions and callouts
  (game interface; difficulty levels; participant flow; FIAS composite
  distribution; FIDS-FIAS mechanism; three-arm design).
- Tables 1–7, Arabic numbering, embedded with callouts.
- JMIR back matter: Acknowledgments, Data Availability, Authors'
  Contributions, Conflicts of Interest, Abbreviations, References
  (numbered in order of citation, [1]–[46]).
