## What the manuscript now contains

- Title with study type suffix ("… Action Research Study" — JMIR convention).
- Structured abstract, 429 words (limit 450): Background / Objective /
  Methods / Results / Conclusions.
- IMRD structure: Introduction (Background, Research Questions, Prior
  Work, Objectives and Contribution) → Methods (Study Design, Ethical
  Considerations, Data Governance, Site and Participants, Definitions,
  the Gamified Intervention, Data Collection, Data Verification and
  Withdrawal Statement, Statistical Analysis) → Results → Discussion
  (incl. Limitations, Practical Implications, Future Work, Conclusions).
- Figures 1–6 embedded in text with sequential captions and callouts
  (game interface; difficulty levels; participant flow; FIAS composite
  distribution; FIDS-FIAS mechanism; three-arm design).
- Tables 1–7, Arabic numbering, embedded with callouts.
- JMIR back matter: Acknowledgments, Data Availability, Authors'
  Contributions, Conflicts of Interest, Abbreviations, References
  (numbered in order of citation, [1]–[46]).

## Submission steps (games.jmir.org)

1. Create an account / log in at https://games.jmir.org and start a new
   submission as an **Original Paper**.
2. Enter metadata: title, both authors with ORCID (create ORCIDs if you
   don't have them — JMIR asks), affiliations, corresponding author
   (sidney.tam@connect.polyu.hk), abstract (paste the structured
   abstract), keywords.
3. Upload the manuscript .docx as the main document. Figures are already
   in the text; JMIR may additionally ask for original high-resolution
   figure files during production — they are ready in
   `figures_submission/` (PNG + TIFF).
4. Ethics: state that the study was conducted under the ethics policy of
   The Hong Kong Polytechnic University with informed consent (matches
   the Ethical Considerations section). If the submission form asks for
   an approval number, use the HSESC reference once obtained — the same
   blocking item as the thesis.
5. **APC**: JMIR Serious Games charges an Article Processing Fee on
   acceptance (check the current fee on the journal's site — typically
   in the US$2,000–3,000 range). Confirm whether PolyU or the
   department covers APCs BEFORE submitting.
6. Cover letter (optional but recommended): 3–4 sentences — the field
   problem (39%/74%/82%), the core finding (70% disengagement, zero
   re-engagement, improvement-yet-abandonment), and why JMIR Serious
   Games (gamified health intervention, older adults, attrition — citing
   the journal's own attrition canon).
