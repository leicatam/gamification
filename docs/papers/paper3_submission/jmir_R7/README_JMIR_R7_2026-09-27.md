## Cover letter (draft for the supervisor's approval — not sent)
Dear Editor,

We submit "Engagement With a Coordination Game Derived From Fall Risk Assessment: Exploratory Observational Study" for consideration in JMIR Formative Research as an original paper. The manuscript reports a compiled engagement record from a March–April 2026 remote deployment of a web-based coordination game derived from a gamified fall risk assessment, across three age bands of adults in Hong Kong. It is a formative, descriptive study: it states its denominators, the limits of its compiled classifications, the unresolved instrument wording, and the ethics chronology, and it makes no causal claim about clinical framing.

The work has not been published and is not under consideration elsewhere. Related Stage-1 and Stage-2 reports are cited and no result is duplicated. Both authors meet ICMJE authorship criteria and approved the submission. The corresponding author is Sidney K. T. Tam (sidney.tam@connect.polyu.hk).
