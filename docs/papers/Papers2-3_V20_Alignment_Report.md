## Paper 3 — the draft could not be adjusted; it was rewritten

The "Key Contexts Draft" was a pre-study scaffold: it presents a
**three-arm Phase-2 study as the paper's confirmatory evidence, and that
study has not been run** (its own §V lists the results sections as
placeholders "until Phase 2 data collection is complete"). Submitting any
adjustment of it would claim evidence that does not exist. What *was* run
is Stage 3: the between-cohort decoupled deployment (N = 120, register
38/41/41).

`Paper3_R1_Stage3_Manuscript.docx` is therefore a full rewrite reporting
the study actually conducted:
- Title: "Decoupling the Clinical Attachment from Gamified Fall Risk
  Assessment: Between-Cohort Evidence for a Frame-Sensitive Account of
  Elderly Abandonment".
- Methods: the decoupling package (recruitment language, no pre-scan, no
  clinical labels, no participant-facing clinical outputs), the eye-leg
  coordination-challenge framing with the scaffold's three-layer design
  logic retained, cohort 38/41/41, preliminary-aggregate basis stated.
- Results: all V20 figures — retention 93.3%/85.4%, returns 65.2%/62.5%,
  endorsement 71.7%, intention 63.3%/56.1%, H0-age nullified (0/38 vs
  16/41, Fisher p < 0.0001; chi-square p < 0.001), non-return
  0.0/4.9/14.6%, Wilson CIs, commitment gap 29.3 pp.
- Discussion: frame-sensitive vs dispositional (calibrated association
  language), WTA/WTP reading of the age gradient (marked inferential —
  no elicitation was performed), commitment gap → adaptive learning.
- The scaffold's WTA/WTP formalism, two-regime (endowment vs learning)
  proposition and three-arm design are retained as theory and future
  work respectively — not as evidence.
- The scaffold's "Adaptive Learning Model" four-component section was
  not carried over as a claimed contribution (it overlaps the thesis's
  adaptive-learning framework; the paper points to the framework
  instead). If you want it in the paper as a proposal section, say so
  and I will add it with claim discipline.
