# Arc 5 — Risk, Liability, Insurance

Outline only. Content drafted in a later phase.

Sections planned:

1. Directors' duties under Cap. 622 (duty of care, fiduciary duty, statutory duties)
2. The limited-liability shield — what protects it, what pierces it (commingling, fraud, etc.)
3. Professional indemnity insurance — when, how much, exclusions
4. D&O insurance for a sole-director Ltd — usually overkill, when it isn't
5. Agent-action liability — who answers when the agent gets it wrong
6. Common pitfalls — missed audit deadlines, mixed personal/company funds, ignored SCR updates
7. Mastery check — three risk scenarios, choose the right response
