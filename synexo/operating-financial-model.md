# Synapep — Operating Financial Model (bottom-up)

> 5-year plan from **July 2026** (prep, training, regulatory applications, cGMP/ISO). **First production readiness February 2027.** Vial-based razor-and-blades consumable model. Built from founder operating inputs; 2028–2030 clinic counts are assumptions. Illustrative; USD.

## Assumptions

| Item | Value |
|---|---|
| Ex-factory price / vial | $30–35 (base $32.50); same Korea-direct & overseas distributor |
| Cost / vial | $7 = Eyesel bottoming $4 + exosome/peptide+raw material $3 |
| Gross profit / vial | $25.50 (78.5% margin) |
| Vials / clinic / month | 100 (MOQ 300/order → ~quarterly stocking) |
| Production capacity | 60,000 vials/month (= 720,000/yr → ~600 clinics) with 4 lab + 2 shared staff |
| End-user price / vial | $60–70 (clinic ~50% margin) |
| Team | 4 Exosome Lab + 2 support (shared by Eyesel); testing & facilities shared by Eyesel |

## Unit economics

- **Per vial (Synapep):** price $32.50 − cost $7 = **$25.50 GP (78.5%)**.
- **Per clinic / year (Synapep):** 1200 vials × $32.50 = **$39,000 revenue / $30,600 GP**.
- **Channel (clinic):** buys $30–35, sells end-user $60–70 → ~50% clinic margin (~$3,250/month gross per clinic).

## Capacity, funding & notes

- **Capacity headroom:** 2030 at ~69% of the 60K/month line (498,000 vials); beyond ~600 clinics needs a second line.
- **Capital efficiency:** **EBITDA-positive from 2027** (first production year). Cumulative EBITDA turns positive during 2027, so the **$1.0M kickoff funds prep + working capital** to self-sustaining — no large survival round required.
- **Operating leverage:** the 4-lab + 2-shared team is fixed to 60K/month, so margin expands as clinics grow (lab headcount flat; growth is sales/registration).
- **Working capital:** MOQ 300/order + distributor terms imply inventory + receivables; size a WC buffer in the raise.
- **Scope:** vial (consumable) revenue only — applicator device placement is excluded (placed/loaned or priced separately; confirm).
- **Assumptions to confirm:** 2028–2030 clinic counts (220/350/480), base price, OpEx (Korea-loaded salaries), and shared-services charge from Eyesel.
