This is precisely the capability set Route A lacks — the entity is thin, but the operator is not.
- **What still needs verification** — Management references and background; the 2024 “dispute between previous shareholders” that preceded the pivot; the investor-network claims; and the internal-papers-vs-public-website narrative gap — a management-integrity question that must be answered before he fronts a listed group. Gate-zero is EXTENDED to cover these; it does not disappear.
