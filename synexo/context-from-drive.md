## What Synexo actually is

**SynExo (Synthetic / Biomimetic Exosome)** — a salmon-derived *synthetic recombinant
exosome* platform that carries plasmid-DNA cargo encoding growth factors (EGF, bFGF, VEGF)
and mRNA therapeutics into cells, which then transiently produce the healing proteins. It is
**not a "peptide" per se** — it is a delivery vehicle. Peptides/proteins are part of the
cargo and of the AI-designed actives (see below).

**Core technologies (these are the user's "IT-exo" and "ai.peptide"):**

| User's term | Real name | What it is |
|-------------|-----------|------------|
| IT-exo | **IT-EXO®** | Immune-Tolerant exosome tech: HLA-G protein attached to the exosome surface acts as a "stealth"/immune shield (engages ILT2, ILT4, KIR2DL4; suppresses NK/T-cell attack). Lets results compound over time without immune decline. |
| ai.peptide | **CodeLife.AI** (AI design platform) | AI-designed peptides and proteins for functional cosmetics; AI for exosome targeting, cargo-loading simulation, and efficacy/behaviour analysis. |
| Synexo | **SynExo / SYN-EXO** | The synthetic recombinant exosome platform itself. |

**SynExo technical specs (from the product/tech doc):**
- ~20 billion particles/mL; size 30–150 nm (95% in range)
- >95% exosome purity; >60% cargo encapsulation
- Stability: **12 months** (bulk −80 °C); **proprietary stabilisation (lyophilised) yields an
  ambient-/2–8 °C clinic format** → **no −80 °C cold-chain cost** (clarified by founder, 2026-06)
- Cargo: EGF (tissue repair), bFGF (angiogenesis), VEGF (neovascularisation); mRNA (8 h
  intracellular stability, protein detected within 12 h); custom loading per application
- IT-EXO effect: significant cytokine reduction (IL-1β ~1.8→0.8 ng/mL, TNF-α ~0.7→0.4
  ng/mL, sustained ~14 days); >95% cell viability
