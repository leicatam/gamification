# Synapep — International Expansion / Export Strategy

> KFDA/MFDS registration as an **export springboard**, not a Korea-only play. Scope: **Tier 1
> (ASEAN + Hong Kong)** and **Tier 2 (GCC + Greater China)**. Tier 3 (EU/US/Japan) is long-term
> and heavily caveated. Illustrative planning document; verify each pathway with local counsel.

## 1. Thesis — two-speed export

A single Korean approval does not auto-clear other markets, but it is a powerful **anchor**:

- **The applicator device travels widely.** Built on the Korean technical file, the
  microneedling/topical **device** rides **reliance/recognition** pathways into reference-friendly
  markets with modest incremental work.
- **The consumable kit is wrapped per market.** The peptide/exosome **kit's** legal class
  (cosmetic vs medical device vs drug/biologic) **varies by country**, so each market gets the
  right wrapper and **claim discipline**. **Salmon/synthetic (non-human) origin is a strategic
  advantage** — it sidesteps EU/UK bans on *human*-derived exosomes in cosmetics.
- **Claims are the recurring risk.** Korea banned the word "exosome" in cosmetic ads (Jan 2025);
  the US FDA has issued 12 warning letters and approved none. Market everywhere on
  function/observed benefit, not unsubstantiated medical claims.

**Precedent that de-risks this:** Korean salmon-**PDRN** skin boosters (e.g., Rejuran,
MFDS-approved 2014) used Korean approval to export across SEA, the Middle East and Europe — a
near-exact analog to Synapep's model.

## 2. Tier 1 — ASEAN + Hong Kong (reliance-led, fastest)

| Market | How KFDA helps | Kit-classification approach | Notes |
|--------|----------------|-----------------------------|-------|
| **Hong Kong** | MDACS **recognises Korea MFDS as a reference regulator**; Expedited scheme favours ≥2 reference approvals | Medical device listing (voluntary MDACS) / cosmetic for topical | Pair **Korea + Singapore** to hit the ≥2-reference expedited route; light entry |
| **Vietnam** | **Explicitly accepts MFDS certifications** | Device registration via MFDS reliance | Among the most direct reliance routes off a Korean approval |
| **Singapore** | HSA approval is a **regional gateway** (recognised onward in TH/PH/HK) | HSA medical device / cosmetic notification | Strategic 2nd reference approval to unlock the cascade |
| **Thailand** | Reliance route (via Singapore HSA); Korean data supports | Aesthetic devices **are** medical devices (Thai FDA) | Large aesthetics market; K-beauty halo |
| **Malaysia** | ASEAN AMDD/CSDT dossier built on Korean file | Aesthetic devices **designated medical devices** (Jun 2026) | MDA registration |
| **Indonesia / Philippines** | ASEAN AMDD/CSDT cascade | Device or cosmetic notification per claim | One CSDT dossier → multiple filings |

**Mechanism:** build **one ASEAN CSDT dossier** on the Korean technical file; use **Singapore** as
the gateway approval; let **Hong Kong/Vietnam** lead on direct MFDS reliance.

## 3. Tier 2 — GCC + Greater China

| Market | How KFDA helps | Kit-classification approach | Notes |
|--------|----------------|-----------------------------|-------|
| **Saudi Arabia (SFDA)** | SFDA is the **GCC regional reference**; KFDA + CE accelerate the TFA route | Medical device (SFDA MDMA) | Country-of-origin approval not mandatory, but reference approvals speed review; SFDA approval then references onward across GCC |
| **UAE (MOHAP)** | Reliance weights recognised approvals (CE strongest; KFDA supportive) | Medical device registration | High-spend aesthetics market; gateway to wider Gulf |
| **Taiwan (TFDA)** | Accepts **Korean clinical data** to support submissions | Medical device | Mature aesthetics market |
| **China (NMPA)** | Large but slow; use faster on-ramps | **Hainan cross-border** / topical-cosmetic wrapper first, NMPA device later | Biggest prize, longest timeline; stage entry |

## 4. Sequence & operating requirements

| Phase | Markets | Trigger |
|-------|---------|---------|
| Anchor | **Korea (MFDS)** | Device-family + first kit registered; anchor clinics live |
| Wave 1 (2027–28) | **Hong Kong, Vietnam, Singapore** | Korean approval + Singapore reference secured |
| Wave 2 (2028–29) | **Thailand, Malaysia, Indonesia, Philippines; Saudi, UAE** | ASEAN CSDT dossier; SFDA TFA |
| Wave 3 (2029–30) | **Taiwan, China (Hainan → NMPA)** | Regional revenue base + capital from Series A |

**Each market needs:** a **local responsible person / authorised distributor**, a per-market
**claims map**, and label/registration localisation. **Cold-chain caveat:** −80 °C international
distribution is even harder than domestic — this **reinforces the reformulation-to-2–8 °C action
item** (see business plan §12). Solve stability before Wave 1.

## 5. Why this strengthens the investment case

- Converts a single-country plan into a **multi-market clinic TAM**, so the clinic ramp
  (150 → 1,000) is spread across geographies rather than a heroic single-country target.
- Strengthens **Lens A** market size and the **Series A** narrative (regional platform, not a
  Korea SKU).
- Keeps the company honest: the **device** scales on reliance; the **kit** scales on disciplined,
  per-market classification — no overclaiming.

## 6. Key risks

- **Kit classification drift** — a market may treat the kit as a drug/biologic (slower/heavier).
- **Exosome scrutiny / advertising limits** — manage with non-human origin + claim discipline.
- **Reliance ≠ automatic** — recognition speeds review but local registration, fees, RP and
  post-market duties still apply.
- **Cold chain across borders** — unresolved −80 °C logistics would cap export feasibility.
