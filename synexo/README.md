## Status: DRAFT v0.4 — AI-personalised medical device pivot

The model moved from selling exosome/peptide **vials** to **AI-personalised aesthetic medical
devices**: an applicator **device** + recurring **personalised treatment kits** (razor-and-blades),
configured by **CodeLife.AI**, launching **Korea → Hong Kong** via the fast "modified device" /
MDACS regulatory paths. The AI engine + a compounding **data flywheel** is the moat and the basis
for a future **data-science company** and institutional-AI funding.

- **Economics:** ~65% blended GM; revenue **$5.3M (2027) → $20.3M → $30.2M → $39.9M (2030)**;
  EBITDA-positive in the launch year.
- **Valuation:** **$5–7M pre-money** ($6M midpoint → $1M = 14.3%); AI/data lens lifts the ceiling
  and reframes the 2028 Series A.
- **Structure:** JV — WBI 70% / Eyesel 30%; standalone at ex-factory.

**Open items:** confirm regulatory split (topical device vs IM drug); head-to-head AI efficacy
evidence + own-model build plan; IP-contribution scope; device ASP/GM, clinic ramp, team.
