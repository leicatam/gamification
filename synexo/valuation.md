# Synapep — Valuation (v0.4)

> USD, illustrative. Base case from `financial-model.md` (device + recurring kits; ~65% blended
> GM; revenue $5.3M→$39.9M 2027–2030). Valuation date mid-2026 (pre-revenue). Synapep valued
> **standalone at the ex-factory line**; distributors (e.g. NeuNova) are non-consolidated.

## Two lenses

The kickoff is priced on **Lens A** (the device/consumables business). **Lens B** (the AI /
data-science company) is the prize that lifts the ceiling and the Series A.

### Lens A — device + consumables business (sets the kickoff price)

Recommend **$5–7M pre-money / $6–8M post-money**, **$6M midpoint → $1.0M = 14.3%**.

| Pre-money | Raise | Post-money | Investor % |
|----------:|------:|-----------:|-----------:|
| $5.0M | $1.0M | $6.0M | 16.7% |
| $6.0M | $1.0M | $7.0M | 14.3% |
| $7.0M | $1.0M | $8.0M | 12.5% |

Three methods reconcile:

| Method | Un-risk-adjusted | Risk-adjusted | Round |
|--------|-----------------:|--------------:|-------|
| Seed convention (IP-owning JV) | — | $5–7M | direct |
| DCF (40% discount, 8× EBITDA exit) | ~$35M EV | ~$5–7M | matches |
| Forward multiple (3–6× recurring med-consumables) | $25–37M PV | $5–8M | matches |

Recurring medical consumables + device + regulatory moat support the **upper half ($6–7M)** and
a higher revenue multiple than commodity cosmetics.

### Lens B — AI / data-science company (the prize; institutional AI capital)

With a proprietary aesthetic-medicine model, an AI-QA/analytics suite, and a compounding **data
flywheel**, the comparable set shifts from medtech to **AI/data platforms**, which command much
higher revenue multiples and attract **institutional AI investors**. Lens B does **not** change
the $1M kickoff price (still pre-revenue, priced on Lens A), but it:

1. raises the **valuation ceiling / exit multiple** in the DCF terminal and forward-multiple;
2. reframes the **2028 Series A** as a **premium, institutional AI round** (not a medtech round);
3. is the headline of the investor narrative.

## VC-method check

Seed investor seeking ~10× wants ~$10M back. If Synapep exits ~2031 at ~$95–130M (8× EBITDA, or
materially higher on a Lens-B AI multiple) and the seed stake is diluted ~1.5–2× by the 2028
round, the investor needs ~11–17% today. $1M at $5–7M pre gives **13–17%** → clears ~10× at the
$6M midpoint, with meaningful upside if Lens B holds.

## Recommendation

Price the **$1.0M kickoff at $6M pre-money (Lens A)**; lead the pitch with **Lens B** (AI moat +
data flywheel + data-science-company vision) as the prize and the Series A thesis. Optionally
test institutional-AI appetite for a larger round at a higher pre — a user decision.
