# Synapep Labs — Model Clarification (exosome & synthetic-biotech aesthetics)

> Grounds the Synapep Labs model in how exosome / synthetic-biotech aesthetic products are actually
> regulated and commercialised, and maps the three engines to that reality. Research-backed and
> deliberately honest about what is validated vs a company working assumption. Illustrative; verify
> each pathway with Korean regulatory counsel.

## 1. The model in one paragraph

Synapep Labs is a **physician co-development lab**: elite Korean aesthetic doctors co-design
**purpose-built products** (personalised to the *physician's clinical case*, not the patient),
which are registered fast and cheaply via a **regulatory modification engine**, then spread through
a **benefit-shared KOL flywheel**. It runs co-equal with an **AI/data moat** (CodeLife.AI + a
compounding outcomes dataset). The output is a **portfolio of doctor-originated SKUs** — an
applicator **device** plus **topical/exosome kits** — sellable across KFDA-recognising Asia.

## 2. What the research validates

- **Korea's "modification engine" is real and strengthening.** In **March 2026** MFDS proposed a
  **risk-based "negative-list"** change-management system: only changes that **materially affect
  safety/performance** need prior approval; other post-approval changes are managed under the
  manufacturer's quality system. MFDS already distinguishes **equivalent / improved / new** devices
  — "improved" (modified) products carry lighter timelines than "new."
- **Topical exosome/peptide products iterate even faster as cosmetics.** **Functional cosmetics
  with the same actives/function** as an approved product are typically processed **same-day with
  no fee**; quasi-drug notifications run ~10 business days. This is the regime where "minor
  formulation change → quick relaunch" genuinely holds.
- **Synthetic / "designer" exosomes are a credible, investable category.** Korean leaders
  **ExoCoBio** (stem-cell exosomes; cosmetics + therapeutics; major exporter) and **Brexogen**
  (first major Korean exosome-therapeutic licensing deal, Feb 2025) anchor the space; **INNO-EXOMA
  EXO-SKIN** is a marketed **synthetic** exosome; the literature describes **designer/engineered
  exosomes** (incl. exosome + botulinum-toxin constructs). **SynExo** (synthetic recombinant,
  salmon-derived) sits squarely in this emerging field.
- **The market is large and regenerative-leaning.** Medical aesthetics ≈ $17–18.5B (2024),
  ~10–13% CAGR; Korea is the global innovation hub for regenerative/skin-booster aesthetics
  (the PDRN/"salmon-DNA" precedent shows Korean-origin biologics exporting across Asia).

## 3. The correction this forces (product taxonomy)

The fast/cheap "**<3% ingredient**" mechanic is a **cosmetic / functional-cosmetic / quasi-drug**
concept more than a "medical device" one. So the portfolio is two regulated objects:

| Object | Regulatory class | Iteration pathway |
|---|---|---|
| Applicator (microneedling/ion) | **Medical device** (KR 10-2654857 anchors the family) | Improved-device / negative-list change |
| Doctor-personalised topical/serum kit (peptide/exosome) | **Functional cosmetic / quasi-drug** (mostly outside device class today) | Same-active functional-cosmetic notification; <3% formulation deltas |
| IM / injectable peptide | **Drug / biologic** (separate, slower) | Full pathway — must not gate launch |

**Implication:** present the SKU factory as a **cosmetic/quasi-drug + device** portfolio, not "20
medical devices." Keep **claim discipline** — Korea **bans "exosome" in cosmetic advertising
(Jan 2025)**; market on function; lean on **non-human (salmon/synthetic) origin** to ease
classification and sidestep EU/US human-exosome bans.

## 4. The "double-edged" doctor economics — making it compliant

Paying the KOL who drives peer adoption is powerful **and** is exactly what anti-kickback regimes
target. The defensible structure (US OIG practice + **Korea's anti-rebate / K-Sunshine** rules):

- **Genuine inventorship** — the physician must materially contribute to a patent claim; mis-naming
  inventors voids the IP and the royalty.
- **Fair market value** — royalty set to the IP's value, supported by an FMV opinion.
- **Decoupled from usage** — payments must **not** correlate with the inventor's own purchasing or
  prescribing volume of the product.
- **Documented & disclosed** — written co-development/IP agreements, transparency filings.

Framed this way, Engine 3 is an **FMV co-invention royalty**, not a usage rebate — a growth flywheel
that survives diligence. Framed loosely, it is a liability. Synapep builds it **compliance-first**.
