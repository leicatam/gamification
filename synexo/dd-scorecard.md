# Synapep — Investor DD Scorecard (technical investor lens)

> One-page diligence read of the **validation-led** Synapep plan, scored by an investor with a
> **chemical-engineering** background entering **aesthetic medicine**. RAG = Red / Amber / Green.
> Illustrative diligence aid, not investment advice. Figures per `financial-model.md` (v0.4).

**Legend:** 🟢 Green = credible / de-risked · 🟡 Amber = plausible but unproven, needs evidence ·
🔴 Red = material gap that must be resolved before scaling/closing.

| # | Category | RAG | Rationale (one line) | Gating DD question |
|---|----------|-----|----------------------|--------------------|
| 1 | Commercial validation | 🟡 | "$25M+ collective sales exposure" is unverified and aggregates affiliated entities; 20+ physician testimony supports *usability*, not efficacy. | Show the $25M at entity/product/channel level and reorder data — how much is *this* product's demand? |
| 2 | Chemistry / formulation | 🟡 | SynExo/IT-EXO treated as settled; the personalisation-vs-"same raw materials" envelope is asserted, not defined. | What is the exact pre-cleared configuration envelope, and is variation within it clean *and* clinically differentiating? |
| 3 | Manufacturing & cold chain | 🔴 | COGS is top-down (20%×EXW + $4.50), no BOM; **−80 °C, 12-mo** stability is unshippable to typical clinics; cold-chain cost absent from P&L. | Reformulate to 2–8 °C or show the cold-chain cost line + BOM + per-batch QC release cost. |
| 4 | Regulatory | 🟡 | Strong segmentation (topical/device fast-path vs IM/drug) is a green-leaning strength; exosome scrutiny + SaMD exposure pull it to amber. | Classification memo (Korea/HK): does the device path survive the personalisation envelope, and how are exosome claims controlled? |
| 5 | AI / data moat | 🟡 | "Own large model" overreaches for a 2→12 FTE team; real asset is a structured, consented outcomes dataset gated on clinic data rights. | Are data-rights clauses in the clinic contract, and what does CodeLife v1 actually do today (demo)? |
| 6 | Financials | 🟡 | Ramp (0→150 clinics yr 1) is aggressive — plan itself calls 150 "upside"; early-year margins likely overstated (QC/cold-chain fixed costs). | Rebuild base off anchor-clinic actuals: real kits/clinic/month + reorder cohorts. |
| 7 | Valuation / deal | 🟡 | $7M ask leans on validation that is "still to be packaged"; floor (device/consumables) supports ~$5–6M pre today. | What price holds *before* the validation pack exists — and what re-rates it at Series A? |
| 8 | IP & JV | 🟡 | Most patents are *applications*; microneedling device (KR 10-2654857) is *granted* (good); JV IP contribution undocumented. | Show the WBI IP assignment/exclusive-license schedule into the JV. |
| 9 | Team / capital adequacy | 🟡 | $1M must cover reg + a prospective study + mfg/QC validation + AI v1 + launch — thin; Series A dependency is high. | Which milestones can $1M *actually* hit before a financing gap appears? |
| 10 | Market reach & export | 🟡 | KFDA is a real export springboard (HK/Vietnam reliance, ASEAN cascade, GCC/SFDA) — but the *kit's* classification varies per market and exosome claims are restricted. | Per-market: is the kit a cosmetic, device, or drug — and does salmon/synthetic origin clear the EU/cosmetic bans? |

## Overall verdict

**Conditional interest — diligence yes, their price no.** The narrative is fundable and unusually
disciplined (claim-language control, regulatory segmentation, validation-pack thinking). But the
incremental value rests on **unverified commercial claims** and the parts a chemical engineer is
best placed to judge — **cold chain, COGS BOM, formulation/personalisation, exosome regulatory
status** — are the least evidenced.

- **What I'd underwrite today:** a validated-ecosystem, regulatory-segmented **consumables
  business with a credible data-upside option**.
- **Price:** **$5–6M pre, milestone-gated** (not $7M, not the AI/data-company multiple yet).
- **Single biggest unlock:** resolve **cold chain + COGS BOM (item 3)** and produce the
  **validation pack** (items 1, 2, 8). Do that and amber→green across the board.
